This file describes the data in the data download for Chapter 2 of the Pacific Salmon Treaty: Skeena and Nass River Sockeye.

The following is a description of included columns:

	Chapter: 
		The chapter of the Pacific Salmon Treaty the record pertains to. For the current data set, this value should always be 2. 

	Region:
		The river of origin that the data pertains to. The two rivers included in this data set are Skeena River and Nass River.

	Species:
		The species that the data pertains to. For the current data set, this should only include sockeye salmon.

	Year:
		The year that the salmon returned to their river of origin, or were caught.

	Spawners:
		The total number of salmon reaching their spawning ground. For Skeena River, this includes surplus enhanced fish that do not spawn.

	Harvest:
		The total number of salmon caught by region, species, and year.

	Harvest Rate:
		The exploitation rate, calculated by dividing the harvest by the total run size for that year.
	
	Returns:
		The total run size, which equals the number of spawners plus the harvest.
