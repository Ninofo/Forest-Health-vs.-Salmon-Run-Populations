This file describes the data in the data download for Chapter 8 of the Pacific Salmon Treaty: Yukon River Chinook and Chum Salmon.

The following is a description of included columns:

	Chapter: 
		The chapter of the Pacific Salmon Treaty the record pertains to. For the current data set, this value should always be 8. 

	Region:
		The region the record pertains to. For the current data set, this is always Yukon River.

	Species:
		The species that the data pertains to. For the current data set, this includes Chinook and chum salmon.

	Year:
		The year that the salmon returned to their river of origin, or were caught.

	Spawners:
		The total number of Canadian-origin salmon reaching their spawning ground.

	Harvest:
		The total number of salmon caught by region and year based on catch reports from various fisheries in Canada and Alaska.

	Harvest Rate:
		The exploitation rate, calculated by dividing the harvest by the total run size for that year.

	Returns:
		The total run size, which equals the number of spawners plus the harvest.
